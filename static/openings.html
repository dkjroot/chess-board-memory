How to practise

Select one of your pieces and its destination, or drag it. Tab and Enter work too.

Click a playlist name to bring it onto the board without changing the checkboxes or mode. Shuffle chooses from checked openings. Repeat cycles through the checked variations of the opening and side on the board. Repeat 1 keeps that exact variation, and switches on automatically after a mistake. Choose another icon whenever you're ready to mix things up.

A mistake shows your move with a flashing red outline, then sends you back to the start. One mistake highlights the piece when you reach that position again; a second adds a ghost destination. Get the move right once and its hints clear.

White and Black repertoires have separate checkboxes and may cover different branches. Your side is shown beside each playlist name and above the board. The board faces your side and the opponent moves automatically. Move ideas appear after the move is played, with your opponent’s ideas above the board and yours below. The latest explained move for each side stays until it is replaced or you restart. Layout controls adjust the playlist width and board size, and are saved in this browser. The main bar sets a shared limit of up to N moves per side. Shorter lines finish at their own length. Finish the enabled lines at that depth before adding a move. The London line has eight White moves; Queen’s Gambit Accepted and Declined each have five. Your menu choices, mode and shared depth are remembered in this browser; hints and run progress reset when you reload.
