How to practise

Select a White piece and its destination, or drag it. Tab and Enter work too.

Click a playlist name to bring it onto the board without changing the checkboxes or mode. Shuffle chooses from checked openings. Repeat cycles through the checked variations of the opening on the board. Repeat 1 keeps that exact variation, and switches on automatically after a mistake. Choose another icon whenever you're ready to mix things up.

A mistake shows your move with a flashing red outline, then sends you back to the start. One mistake highlights the piece when you reach that position again; a second adds a ghost destination. Get the move right once and its hints clear.

Finish the enabled lines at one depth before adding a move. The London line has eight White moves; Queen’s Gambit Accepted and Declined each have five. Your menu choices, mode and depths are remembered in this browser; hints and run progress reset when you reload.
