How to practise

Study the position, then rebuild it on the empty board. Drag pieces from the tray, or select a piece and tap a square. Use Tab and Enter for keyboard placement.

Select a placed piece to move it. Use “Return selected” or Delete to put it back in the tray. Pieces are identified by colour and type.

These are memory patterns, so positions do not have to be legal chess positions.
