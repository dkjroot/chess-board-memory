How to practise

Remember the whole position. The question appears only after the pieces disappear. Rebuild the position or mark its threats, as asked. Drag from the tray, or select a piece or symbol and tap a square. Use Tab and Enter for keyboard placement.

Tap with the same symbol to remove a mark; use a different symbol to change it. For two-colour questions: red X means threatened by White, blue circle means threatened by Black. Each puzzle has at least one answer.

White pawns attack towards higher ranks; Black towards lower ranks. Don't mark defended pieces of the attacker's own colour.

For memory answers, select a placed piece to move it. “Return selected” or Delete puts it back in the tray.

These are practice patterns, so positions do not have to be legal chess positions. Pinned pieces still count as attacking.
